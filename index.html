About

I am an MSc Data Science student at EPFL, working at the intersection of computer vision, large-scale multimodal learning, medical imaging, and foundation models. My current work at VILAB focuses on building scalable image and video datasets using distributed pseudolabeling and tokenization pipelines for downstream visual learning.

Previously, I completed a double BSc in Data Science and Engineering and Engineering Physics at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya through CFIS, UPC's selective double-degree engineering program. For my bachelor's thesis, I completed a year-long research internship at the NYU Courant Institute, supervised by Prof. Carlos Fernández-Granda, on foundation models for brain MRI.

My main interest is representation learning: how visual models build internal abstractions of geometry, motion, and semantics from raw data, and how these representations can generalize beyond the exact distribution they were trained on.

Foundation Models for Brain MRI

NYU Courant Institute Sep 2024 - May 2025

Built a large-scale brain MRI dataset with anatomical and clinical labels, pretrained ViT models with MAE and DINO, and designed a supervised segmentation pretraining stage to add anatomical priors to image embeddings.

Bachelor thesis supervised by Prof. Carlos Fernández-Granda. The thesis is available here, and the related paper is currently under review at npj Digital Medicine.
