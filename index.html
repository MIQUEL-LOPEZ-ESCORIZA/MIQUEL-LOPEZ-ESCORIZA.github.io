About

I am an MSc Data Science student at EPFL, working at the intersection of large-scale multimodal learning, medical imaging, and foundation models. My current work at VILAB focuses on building scalable image and video datasets with pseudolabeling and tokenization pipelines for downstream visual learning.

Previously, I completed a double BSc in Data Science and Engineering and Engineering Physics at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya through CFIS, UPC's selective double-degree engineering program. I also spent a year at NYU Courant Institute working on foundation models for brain MRI.
